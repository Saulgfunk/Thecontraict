MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Master Supply Agreement (this "Agreement") is made as of January 10, 2025 (the "Effective Date") between Apex Components Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Supplier"), and Brightline Manufacturing LLC ("Buyer").

ARTICLE 1 - SCOPE
Supplier shall manufacture and deliver the products listed in Exhibit A (the "Products") pursuant to purchase orders issued by Buyer.

ARTICLE 2 - TERM
Section 2.1 The initial term of this Agreement is one (1) year from the Effective Date.
Section 2.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for additional one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.
Section 2.3 Buyer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Supplier.

ARTICLE 3 - PRICING AND PAYMENT
Section 3.1 Prices are as set out in Exhibit B and are fixed for the initial term. Thereafter, Supplier may adjust prices once per contract year on January 10 by giving at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice, with any increase capped at 4%.
Section 3.2 Supplier shall invoice Buyer monthly, on the last business day of each month, for Products delivered during that month. Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt (net 45). Estimated annual purchases are USD 2,400,000.

ARTICLE 4 - WARRANTY
Supplier warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for eighteen (18) months from the date of delivery.

ARTICLE 5 - NOTICES
Notices shall be given in writing by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set out on the signature page, and shall be deemed received three (3) business days after mailing.

ARTICLE 6 - GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
