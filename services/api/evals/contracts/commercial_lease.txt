LEASE OF COMMERCIAL PREMISES

PARTIES
(1) Harbourside Properties Limited (the "Landlord"); and
(2) Meridian Legal LLP (the "Tenant").

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
In this Lease, "Quarter Days" means 25 March, 24 June, 29 September and 25 December in each year.

2. TERM
2.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for a term of five (5) years from and including 1 July 2023 (the "Term Commencement Date") and expiring on 30 June 2028.
2.2 This Lease does not renew automatically.

3. RENT
3.1 The Tenant shall pay the initial annual rent of GBP 120,000 (exclusive of VAT) by equal quarterly payments of GBP 30,000 in advance on the Quarter Days.
3.2 The first payment shall be made on the Term Commencement Date in respect of the period up to the next Quarter Day.

4. RENT REVIEW
The rent shall be reviewed on 1 July 2026 (the "Review Date") to the open market rent at that date, provided that the rent shall not be reduced. Either party may serve a written notice to initiate the review at any time after 1 January 2026.

5. OPTION TO RENEW
5.1 The Tenant may extend the term for a further period of five (5) years by giving the Landlord written notice not less than six (6) months and not more than twelve (12) months before 30 June 2028.
5.2 The option in clause 5.1 is personal to Meridian Legal LLP.

6. INSURANCE
The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than GBP 5,000,000 and provide evidence of renewal of the policy to the Landlord within fourteen (14) days of each renewal date. The current policy expires on 31 January 2027.

7. NOTICES
Any notice under this Lease shall be in writing and delivered by hand or sent by recorded delivery to the address of the recipient stated above. A notice sent by recorded delivery is deemed served on the second (2nd) Business Day after posting.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease and any dispute arising from it are governed by the law of England and Wales.
