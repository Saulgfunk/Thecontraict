MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made on 2 May 2025 between Anadolu Teknoloji A.S., Istanbul ("Company A") and Fjordline Analytics AS, Oslo ("Company B").

1. PURPOSE
The parties wish to explore a potential joint venture (the "Purpose") and will exchange confidential information for that Purpose.

2. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential and use it only for the Purpose.

3. TERM
3.1 This Agreement commences on the date above and remains in force for two (2) years.
3.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving not less than thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.
3.3 The obligations in clause 2 survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

4. RETURN OF INFORMATION
Within ten (10) days of a written request, the receiving party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information.

5. NO PAYMENT
No fees are payable under this Agreement.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Turkey. The Istanbul courts have jurisdiction.
